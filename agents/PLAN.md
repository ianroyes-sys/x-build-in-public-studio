# Plan (template)

Goal: grow the account organically by showing real work, so people who hire builders notice.

- Post concrete systems with proof: a recording of the thing running beats a description of it.
- Two posts a day at most while the account is new; slots are 8:30, 12:30 and 17:30 local.
- Video and images go in the post. Links go in a reply, because X shows posts with outside links to fewer people.
- Answer every reply in the first hour. That hour decides whether a post leaves your followers.
- Spend about 20 minutes a day replying under builders with 10–60K followers, saying what you built.
- Skip hype, money flexes and engagement bait.
