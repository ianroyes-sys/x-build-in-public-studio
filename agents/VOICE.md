# Voice (template)

Replace this with how you actually write. x-studio reads it at the start of every run.

- lowercase is fine. short lines. one idea per post.
- the claim and the number go in line one ("cut my build from 9 min to 2").
- plain words. say what broke and how you fixed it.
- no em dashes, no hashtags, no "not X but Y", no motivational closers, no "here's the thing".
- never name clients, employers, or anything in `data/x/denylist.txt`.
- if the media shows demo data, say so.

Paste three or four of your own best posts below as the register to match.
